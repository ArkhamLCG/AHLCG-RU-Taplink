]]> The complete series of Terror at the End of Time from Dark Horse Comics has been collected into a trade paperback and is available now wherever you buy your comics and graphic novels. The trade paperback collects all four issues of the mini-series, featuring Jenny Barnes and Joe Diamond as the pair search for Jenny’s missing sister, Izzie. The series is written by Cullen Bunn, with art by Andrea Mutti, and cover art from Rafael Albuquerque.

From Dark Horse:

Eisner-nominated master of horror Cullen Bunn brings Arkham Horror—the hair-raising 1920s mystery and adventure game—to the world of comics with Arkham Horror: The Terror at the End of Time.

Life was all just one big game to adventuring socialite Jenny Barnes. But Jenny’s world turned topsy-turvy when she and her sister Izzie were attacked by a dangerous cult. Although Jenny thwarted the cultists sacrificial endeavors and battled the eldritch monsters at their command, the high priestess Abigail Olmstead fled—and took Izzie with her.

Desperate to find her sister, Jenny seeks the help of private investigator Joe Diamond. Joe may not understand her story, but he understands loss. Together, Joe and Jenny will follow a perilous path—fighting unimaginable horrors, journeying to strange worlds, and risking their lives and sanity—in search of Izzie. But the greatest terror awaits them somewhere far beyond the town of Arkham, Massachusetts…

Collects Arkham Horror: The Terror at the End of Time #1–#4.

As well as the complete comic series, the trade paperback collects the bonus material from all four issues of the comic, including a parallel Investigator card for Jenny for use in Arkham Horror: The Card Game, a church basement encounter and map for Arkham Horror: The Roleplaying Game, the opening passages of the mini-gamebook adventure The Magpie & The Bat, and more.

To find out more, visit the Dark Horse website, or pick up a copy wherever you find comics and graphic novels.

A previous version of this article incorrectly mentioned a parallel Investigator card for Roland Banks. The trade paperback includes a parallel Investigator card for Jenny Barnes only.

]]> Arkham Horror meets the silver screen this week, with Film Fatale coming soon for Arkham Horror: The Card Game, while today sees the release of the latest Arkham Horror omnibus from Aconyte Books. Visions & Nightmares collects three novels by Rosemary Jones – Mask of Silver, The Deadly Grimoire and The Bootlegger’s Dance – that all share some connection to the silver screen in the golden age of movie-making. It’s a real triple bill of horror, as the cover, by Daniel Strange, perfectly captures.

A sunken city. A sleeping sickness. A rising tide.

The time has come. Cthulhu has awakened.

In the dramatic fight to save Arkham, it will take the investigators everything they’ve got to put the dreamer back to rest and save their city from complete annihilation. Arkham will never be the same, and neither will its heroes.

The Drowned City storyline spans multiple games, novels, and official accessories from our partners at Fantasy Flight Games, Z-Man Games, Aconyte Books, and Loot Studios. Check out our Lore pages to discover the investigators, monsters, Ancient Ones, and Other Worlds at the heart of the mystery, and pick up your copy of the Arkham Advertiser for the local paper’s coverage on the impending storms.

Read on to discover what all lies in store for you as you behold the Drowned City in all its wretched and cyclopean glory.

Pore Over the Novels

The chronicle begins with The Forbidden Visions of Lucius Galloway by Carrie Harris. In this prequel novel, poet Lucius Galloway is offered a prestigious position translating a mysterious ancient text at Harvard University, but when he realizes his visions of a sunken city and the tome are connected, he must solve the mystery at the heart of the translation before the hidden figures dogging his footsteps catch up to him.

The saga continues with The Nightmare Quest of April May by Rosemary Jones. In Arkham, a mysterious sleeping sickness has taken hold, and April May, an advertising salesperson at the Arkham Advertiser, is drawn in to a mysterious adventure – alongside some familiar faces from the game.

Stay tuned for the thrilling conclusion to the trilogy in the third and final novel.

Dive Into the Card Game

The centerpiece of the Drowned City storyline is the eponymous The Drowned City Campaign Expansion. In this thrilling expansion, one to four investigators explore the mysterious and deadly city of R’lyeh as it rises from the ocean’s depths. As players venture into the tomb-like ruin, they’ll scale the city’s crumbling foundations, scour the sea floor for alien artifacts, navigate mind-bending machinery and shifting corridors, and discover an ancient history that was drowned with the city itself. Along the way, they’ll experience a unique story that changes based on their chosen investigators, and it all culminates in a climactic battle against Cthulhu himself! Best of all, players only need a copy of the Arkham Horror: The Card Game Revised Core Set to dive into the story.

That being said, players might wish to expand their deckbuilding options with The Drowned City Investigator Expansion, featuring six new investigators for Arkham Horror: The Card Game. Marion Tavares, Lucius Galloway, Michael McGlen, Agatha Crane, Gloria Goldberg, and George Barnaby join the lineup of investigators to help ensure Arkham’s survival. The expansion adds a trove of new player cards and options for deckbuilding, including brand-new “Specialist” cards that play off investigators’ traits.

Explore More Board and Card Games

As a warm-up (or a chaser) to the eldritch adventure of the novels and card game, grab a copy of Arkham Horror Lovecraft Letter, a chilling twist on the classic deduction card game. Delve into the madness of the Cthulhu Mythos as you face eldritch horrors and uncover allies tainted with madness in this gripping battle of wits. Can you maintain your sanity and outwit Cthulhu, or will you succumb to the creeping darkness that lurks within?

And coming soon, Turn of a Page is a wholly digital scenario for Mansions of Madness: Second Edition that ties in narratively with the Drowned City campaign for Arkham Horror: The Card Game. After a group of investigators returns from an expedition to R’lyeh carrying powerful new artifacts, it falls to your investigators to uncover more of the story, claiming those artifacts to defend Arkham against the rising doom. You need only the base game of Mansions of Madness to play, but if you own any of the expansions, the scenario can optionally incorporate that content. Turn of a Page will feature multiple ways to vary the gameplay and map so that there is something new to discover every playthrough.

Study the Horror in Resin

And last but not least, if you are a 3D printing hobbyist, you won’t want to miss the official Rise of Cthulhu statue and dice tower from Loot Studios. You can begin your 3D printing adventure with the free Cthonian Broodling download, and add the monster to your campaigns of Arkham Horror: The Roleplaying Game with this free NPC sheet.

Check out the entire range of Drowned City products below!

]]> If you’ve been following social media, you’ll have seen some pretty big news today from Aconyte Books as part of our Arkham Nights celebration. In case you missed it, here’s a round-up, along with more information on these fantastic upcoming titles.

The Forbidden Visions of Lucius Galloway (Drowned City, Book 1)

Announced last month, today we can reveal the cover for the forthcoming Arkham Horror novel, The Forbidden Visions of Lucius Galloway by Carrie Harris with cover art by Martín M. Barbudo.

The novel serves as a prequel to the upcoming Arkham Horror: The Card Game expansion, The Drowned City, and introduces Lucius Galloway, one of the Investigators featured in the new expansion. What’s more, you can hear author Carrie Harris reading the book’s prologue in the video below, and you can also read the first three chapters online now.

The Forbidden Visions of Lucius Galloway will be published November 19. For more about the book, including pre-order info, visit Aconyte’s website.

The Nightmare Quest of April May (Drowned City, Book 2)

In some of today’s other big news, we can announce that The Forbidden Visions of Lucius Galloway is just the first part of a loose trilogy of novels ushering in the Drowned City, and the second – The Nightmare Quest of April May by Rosemary Jones – is coming Spring 2025!

For more information on this upcoming title, and to make sure you don’t miss any news – including the cover reveal and release date for The Nightmare Quest of April May – make sure you sign up for the Aconyte newsletter.

The Tides of Innsmouth (Investigators Gamebooks #2)

Earlier this year, Aconyte Books released The Darkness Over Arkham, the very first Arkham Horror Investigators Gamebook. Today, we’re pleased to announce the second book in the series is coming Spring 2025.

This second volume in the series is fully compatible with The Darkness Over Arkham, so if you’ve already completed that adventure and you want to play through The Tides of Innsmouth using your chosen Investigator from the first book, you can.

The Tides of Innsmouth will be available for pre-order very soon – follow Aconyte on social media and sign up for the newsletter to make sure you hear first.

The Arkham Advertiser Show Giveaway

If you were lucky enough to be at Gen Con this year, hopefully you managed to pick up a copy of the Arkham Advertiser, a real, printed edition of Arkham’s esteemed paper of record. The Gen Con edition’s front page featured “Grave Rumors” of some strange goings on in Arkham and serves as something of a narrative introduction to EDGE’s The Hungering Abyss roleplaying game starter set – in fact, you can even use it as an in-game prop to immerse your group deeper into the world of Arkham. If you missed it, you can download a digital copy from EDGE’s website.

Well, today, we can announce that Aconyte and EDGE have teamed up for another show giveaway edition of the Arkham Advertiser, which you’ll be able to pick up from PAX Unplugged in Philadelphia (from December 6-8), and Dragonmeet in London (November 30). Don’t worry, though – if you aren’t able to make it to either of these events, this new edition of the Arkham Advertiser will be available to download from EDGE’s site after the shows. You’ll just have to provide your own smell of fresh newsprint.

To stay up-to-date with all the latest news on these and other exciting upcoming titles, sign up for the Aconyte newsletter and follow Aconyte on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X (formerly Twitter) and BlueSky.
